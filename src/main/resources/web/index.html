BEHIND THE PROOF

Designed to prove a match.
Not to make promises.

Every issued notice is signed using Ed25519. The verifier checks the signature against this demo's pinned public key, compares the received content, and checks whether the record expired or was withdrawn.

Important: domain ownership, legal identity, and phishing safety are separate problems. This release makes none of those claims.
